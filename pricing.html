The honest part

Why it's built this way

  • The free tier is free forever. Not a 14-day countdown. The core that helps you function should never be behind a paywall.
  • We never sell your data. There is no ad model here, no data broker, no quiet tracking. Tinfoil Hat mode means what it says.
  • The installed app costs $4.99 once. That pays for the convenience of a native release and helps fund continued development. The web version stays free.
  • Optional AI is priced separately because it creates a real cost every time it runs. The local tools do not require it.
  • Tokens are prepaid, on purpose. No subscription means no shame-tax for the weeks you don't show up. And because it's prepaid, no one — including us — can overspend.
  • We take a fair margin to keep the lights on. Enough for one neurodivergent founder to keep building this, not enough to pretend we're getting rich. That's the deal, out loud.

Divergify does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care. It's a productivity support tool built by someone who needed one.

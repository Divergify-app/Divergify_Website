House rules

Strict on purpose — so it stays a place of acceptance.

  • Be kind. No ableism, no shame, no "just try harder." Executive dysfunction is a lag spike, not a character flaw.
  • Ask before advising. "Want ideas, or just to vent?" Respect different brains, communication styles, and capacities.
  • No hate, harassment, doxxing, or diagnosing others. Disagree all you want — don't be cruel about it.
  • Peer support, not a crisis or medical service. We care, but we're not a substitute for professional help.
  • Correction without humiliation. Most issues get a quiet nudge first. Disappear and come back anytime — no guilt, no decay.

Signal Station is a peer community, not a crisis or medical service. Divergify does not diagnose, treat, or replace professional care. If you're in crisis, please reach out to local emergency or crisis support.

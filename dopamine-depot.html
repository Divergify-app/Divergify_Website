Why this exists

Merch that means something.

Divergify is an independent project built by a neurodivergent founder. No VC. No brand deals. No ads. Dopamine Depot is how the build stays funded — which means every purchase is a direct vote for more tools, more Field Notes, and more Takota.

Designs are low-sensory by default: muted palettes, clean prints, no loud graphics. Because the people buying these shirts probably have enough going on already.

Why this exists

Merch that means something.

Divergify is an independent project built by a neurodivergent founder. No VC. No brand deals. No ads. Dopamine Depot is one way the build stays funded.

Designs should feel recognizable without being humiliatingly specific. No identity callouts as store sections. No glittery diagnostic billboards. Just smart, useful, slightly feral merch.

Divergify

For brains that zig when the world zags.

The world runs on software built for linear brains. You probably don't have one. That's not a flaw — it's a compatibility error.

Divergify is a home base for neurodivergent people. Tools that adapt to fluctuating capacity. Language that doesn't pathologize you. A sidekick named Takota that holds the vision when you can't see it.

Your brain is not the problem. The interface is. Divergify helps you get the pile out of your head, pick the next sane step, and stop treating every unfinished task like courtroom evidence.

The core app is free. No account required. Nothing leaves your device unless you want it to. Premium AI features (Takota live chat via your own key or token packs) are coming next — see Pricing for exactly how that works.

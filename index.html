Divergify

For brains that zig when the world zags.

The world runs on software built for linear brains. You probably don't have one. That's not a flaw — it's a compatibility error.

Divergify is a home base for neurodivergent people. Tools that adapt to fluctuating capacity. Language that doesn't pathologize you. A sidekick named Takota that holds the vision when you can't see it.

The app is live. Start there. The rest of the site — Field Notes, Divergipedia, Dopamine Depot — exists to support the build and explain the thinking behind it.

Start with Brain Dump. If you want to support the build, visit Dopamine Depot.

Your guide

Meet Takota

Takota is the AI sidekick built into Divergify. Not a chatbot. Not a coach. Your external prefrontal cortex — blunt, boundaried, and designed to scaffold without shaming.

Takota reads your capacity, breaks the scary thing into micro-steps, and doesn't take it personally when you disappear for a week.
